# Feature Specification: AI Strategy & Roadmap Page (What We Do)

**Feature Branch**: `feature/TMS-86-what-we-do-ai-strategy-and-roadmap-page`
**Created**: 2026-08-25
**Status**: Draft
**Input**: User description: "consider this feature as TMS-86-ai-strategy-and-roadmap and implement the AI Strategy page in the TechGrit Next.js application. Reference Prototype: `raw-files-v3/TechGrit Website V2.3/TechGrit AI Strategy.dc.html`, treated as the pixel-accurate visual and content source of truth. The AI Strategy page belongs under What We Do in the website header/navigation. Follow the existing architecture used by the other What We Do pages (AI-Accelerated Modernization, Software Product Engineering, Data & AI Engineering, Platform Engineering, Managed Services) — same file/folder structure, component organization, naming, routing, and reusable-component conventions; do not introduce a new architectural pattern when an existing one can be reused. Before creating any new component, inspect `components/ui` and reuse whatever already covers the required functionality; only build a new, genuinely reusable component when nothing existing fits. Use Tailwind CSS only, reusing existing tokens/utilities from `tokens.css`/`globals.css`; only add a new token when the reference genuinely requires a value that doesn't already exist. Match the reference prototype pixel-for-pixel across typography, spacing, containers, grids, cards, buttons, colors, borders, radii, shadows, icons, and images, with no redesign/approximation. Reproduce the reference's responsive behavior (not a uniform desktop shrink) across mobile, tablet, laptop, and desktop using the project's existing canonical breakpoints. Visually verify the implementation against the reference at all four viewport tiers after building, iterating until there is no noticeable difference. Keep the implementation minimal — no unnecessary abstractions, duplicate components/styles, unused props/imports, new dependencies, or changes to unrelated pages/components."

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Evaluate the fractional CTO offering end-to-end (Priority: P1)

A founder, CEO, or board member evaluating whether their company needs senior technology leadership lands on the AI Strategy & Roadmap page — from site navigation, a related "What We Do" page, or a direct link — and reads through the hero promise, the four CTO-as-a-Service capability pillars, and the five-stage engagement flow, to decide whether TechGrit's fractional-CTO model fits their situation before booking a session.

**Why this priority**: This is the entire reason the page exists — it is TechGrit's primary sales asset for its CTO-advisory / AI-strategy service line. Without this flow working, the page delivers no value regardless of what else is built.

**Independent Test**: Load the page standalone and verify a reader can, without leaving the page, understand what "CTO-as-a-Service" means at TechGrit, see the four named capability pillars (Tech & AI Strategy, Software Architecture, Team Management & Leadership, Quality Assurance & Control) with their supporting bullet points, and see the five-stage engagement flow (Diagnose, Roadmap, Execute, Measure, Coach) — this alone constitutes a usable, demonstrable page.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a visitor arrives on the page, **When** the hero section renders, **Then** they see the "Service 06 · CTO Advisory" eyebrow, the headline "Leadership guidance to implement a technical roadmap that hits business goals." (with "technical roadmap that hits business goals." rendered as the gradient-accented phrase), the supporting paragraph, both hero CTAs ("Book a Strategy Session", "See CTO capabilities"), and the right-side highlight card showing only a static image with no stat tiles or caption line — with the reference's original four stat tiles (Advisors, Engagement, Roadmap, Outcomes) and its "AI IMPACT™ · 4D™ · PRISM™ frameworks" caption line not rendered anywhere on or around the card (per FR-004).
2. **Given** a visitor scrolls past the hero, **When** they reach the "Achieve your business goals with senior technology leadership on tap." intro section, **Then** they see the section's heading and paragraph on one side and the "When teams need us" chip list (six trigger chips) on the other.
3. **Given** a visitor reaches the "CTO-as-a-Service capabilities" section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see the section heading "Four pillars. One executive partner." followed by exactly four capability cards (Tech & AI Strategy, Software Architecture, Team Management & Leadership, Quality Assurance & Control), each with its numbered/disciplined label ("01 · Strategy" through "04 · Quality"), heading, lead paragraph, and bullet list, matching the reference verbatim.
4. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Diagnose. Roadmap. Execute. Measure. Coach." section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see the five ordered stages (Diagnose, Roadmap, Execute, Measure, Coach) each with its number, title, and description.

### User Story 2 - Understand why the approach matters and who it's for (Priority: P2)

A visitor who is already sold on the need for fractional technology leadership but is evaluating *why TechGrit specifically* reads the "The CTO you can afford" tiles and the "Founders. Boards. Scaling technology orgs." cards to confirm TechGrit understands both their situation (independent, cost-conscious, AI-native) and has relevant experience with organizations like theirs (startups, scale-ups, PE/VC portfolios, enterprise programs) before proceeding further.

**Why this priority**: This section builds the trust and differentiation needed to convert an already-interested visitor, but the page is still functional and valuable without a reader reaching this far (P1 covers the core pitch).

**Independent Test**: With only the hero, intro, capabilities, and lifecycle sections present, add the "Why" tiles and "Who we advise" cards and verify each renders independently with correct content, without depending on any interactive state from earlier sections.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Why leaders choose TechGrit" section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see all six tiles (Board-grade thinking, Delivery on the same team, AI-native leadership, Fractional flexibility, Independent & honest, Cost-conscious) with icon, heading, and description.
2. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Founders. Boards. Scaling technology orgs." section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see four non-clickable cards (Startup Founders, Scale-ups, PE / VC Portfolio, Enterprise Programs) in that order, each with an icon, heading, and description, matching the reference's own plain (non-linked) card treatment for all four.

---

### User Story 3 - Resolve open questions and convert (Priority: P3)

A visitor with specific procedural concerns (how this compares to a full-time CTO hire, what an engagement looks like, hiring a permanent CTO, M&A/technical due diligence, whether TechGrit also delivers the roadmap) expands the FAQ accordion to get direct answers, optionally checks related services, and then acts on a CTA (books a strategy session or explores Discovery Sprints) or navigates to a related service page.

**Why this priority**: This closes remaining objections and drives the conversion action, but is only reached by visitors who have already engaged with P1/P2 content — it is the funnel's final step, not its entry point.

**Independent Test**: Render just the FAQ, related-services, and closing CTA sections and verify each FAQ item expands/collapses independently, related-service links are correct, and both CTA buttons point to their respective destinations.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the FAQ section is visible, **When** a visitor clicks a closed question, **Then** that question's answer expands in place (the first question, "How does CTO-as-a-Service compare with hiring a full-time CTO?", is expanded by default) without affecting the state of other questions.
2. **Given** the "Related services" section, **When** it renders, **Then** it shows six linked service cards in a three-across grid (AI-Accelerated Modernization, Software Product Engineering, Data & AI Engineering, Platform Engineering, Managed Services, Startups).
3. **Given** the closing CTA band, **When** it renders, **Then** it shows the heading "Turn your AI ambition into a plan you can execute.", the supporting paragraph, and both the primary ("Book a Strategy Session") and secondary ("Explore Discovery Sprints") actions.

### Edge Cases

- What happens on narrow viewports (mobile) where the hero's two-column layout, the two/four-across capability and industry-card grids, the six-across why grid, and the five-across lifecycle strip cannot fit side by side? Each must collapse to a readable single- or two-column stack rather than overflowing horizontally or shrinking text below a legible size.
- What happens when a visitor navigates directly to `#capabilities` (the "See CTO capabilities" hero link) via a bookmarked or shared URL? The target section must be reachable and not obscured by the sticky site header.
- What happens if a visitor expands multiple FAQ items in sequence? Each item's expand/collapse state must be independent of the others (no accordion-style forced-collapse of previously opened items).
- What happens to the "Founders. Boards. Scaling technology orgs." cards, all four of which are non-interactive in the reference (no destination for any of Startup Founders, Scale-ups, PE / VC Portfolio, or Enterprise Programs)? All four must render as plain, non-clickable cards (no hover-lift/pointer affordance implying a destination that doesn't exist), matching the reference's own use of a `<div>` card (not an `<a>`) for every card in this grid.
- What happens to the four hero stat tiles and the hero card's decorative treatment once the right-side content is replaced with an image, per FR-004? The card's size, position, border radius, and framing must remain visually equivalent to the reference even though its contents changed from stat tiles (plus a caption line) to a plain image with no caption, and the image must not distort or crop awkwardly at any supported viewport width.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The page MUST present all page copy — headings, eyebrows, paragraphs, card titles, bullet lists, chip labels, FAQ questions/answers, CTA labels, and link labels — exactly as written in the reference prototype, with no rewriting, rephrasing, shortening, expanding, reordering, or added/removed content (excluding the hero stat tiles/caption removal covered by FR-004).
- **FR-002**: The page MUST reuse the site's existing shared Header and Footer components without modification or duplication of either component's code, and MUST reuse existing `components/ui/` primitives — `Hero`, `ContentBlock`, `GlassCard` (`serviceCapability` variant), `ProcessSteps`, `Faq`, `IconTile` (for the related-services compact cards), and `final-cta` — wherever their shape already matches this page's sections, in place of building new one-off equivalents.
- **FR-003**: The page MUST render, in reference order: hero, "Achieve your business goals with senior technology leadership on tap." intro/chips block, four-capability "Four pillars. One executive partner." grid, five-stage "Diagnose. Roadmap. Execute. Measure. Coach." lifecycle strip, six-tile "The CTO you can afford — and the outcomes you couldn't before." grid, four-card "Founders. Boards. Scaling technology orgs." grid, five-item FAQ accordion, six-card "Related services" grid, and closing CTA band.
- **FR-004**: The hero section's right-side highlight card MUST display only a static image — `public/samples/dm-scalability.png` (per Clarifications and Assumptions) — with no caption line beneath it — with no rendering of the reference's four stat tiles (Advisors, Engagement, Roadmap, Outcomes) or its "AI IMPACT™ · 4D™ · PRISM™ frameworks" caption line anywhere on or around the card — and without distorting the image's aspect ratio at any supported viewport width, matching the sibling "What We Do" pages' own hero-card image-replacement treatment (chrome, dimensions, position, border radius, spacing) via the shared `Hero` component's `mediaFill` media slot.
- **FR-005**: Every interactive affordance present in the reference MUST work in the implementation: each FAQ item independently expands/collapses (first item expanded by default); the "See CTO capabilities" hero link scrolls to the capabilities section without that section being hidden under the sticky header; and all CTA, card, and related-service links navigate to their intended destination (or a page-appropriate equivalent where the destination does not yet exist as a built route).
- **FR-006**: The page's visual presentation (typography scale, weight, line-height, letter-spacing, colors, gradients, spacing, card/section dimensions, border radii, shadows, and icons) MUST match the reference prototype at the reference's desktop width, with no visually approximated values where the reference specifies an exact one.
- **FR-007**: The page's layout MUST adapt responsively across desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile widths, using the project's existing canonical breakpoints (`lg`=1140px, `md`=960px, `sm`=560px) to reproduce the reference's own breakpoint behavior (e.g., multi-column grids collapsing to fewer columns, then to a single column; the hero's two-column split stacking on narrow viewports) rather than a uniform shrink of the desktop layout or a newly invented pixel breakpoint.
- **FR-008**: This phase MUST implement static content only — no network requests, data fetching, CMS integration, or client-side data-derived state — while remaining structured (page-local `_data/types.ts` content shape, matching the sibling pages' own initial-build data organization) so that content can later be supplied dynamically without a rebuild of the page's visual structure, consistent with every "What We Do" page in this codebase having itself first been built statically before any CMS integration followed later as separate, dedicated work (per Assumptions).
- **FR-009**: No functional requirement in this specification requires modifying any page, component, or configuration outside what is needed to build this new page and the minimal navigation-pointer updates in FR-010.
- **FR-010**: The site's existing "What We Do → AI Strategy & Roadmap" entry points MUST resolve to the new page at the route `/what-we-do/ai-strategy-roadmap` once built. Concretely: (a) the footer's "What We Do" link config (`cms/api/footer.ts`) MUST have its "AI Strategy & Roadmap" entry's `href` updated from `/services#svc-strategy` to `/what-we-do/ai-strategy-roadmap`; and (b) the header's mega-menu mapping (`cms/api/header.ts`'s `toMegaGroup`), which already special-cases the "AI-Accelerated Modernization", "Software Product Engineering", "Data and AI Engineering", "Platform Engineering", and "Managed Services" entries to override their stale CMS-supplied links, MUST gain an equivalent sixth special case for the "AI Strategy & Roadmap" entry pointing to the same new route — following the exact precedent already established in that file. Live CMS content, the rest of the `/services` page, and all other navigation entries remain untouched.
- **FR-011**: This feature MUST NOT introduce any new shared `components/ui/` primitive. Every section this page needs (hero with its image-media treatment, intro/chips content block, capability card, numbered process step, icon/text tile, FAQ accordion, and closing CTA band) already has a matching, unmodified `components/ui/` component established by the sibling "What We Do" pages; the "Why leaders choose TechGrit" tiles and the fully-non-linked "Founders. Boards. Scaling technology orgs." cards are implemented as page-local compositions rather than new shared primitives, matching the exact precedent the sibling pages already set for their own equivalent sections. This page's own build work is limited to page-local composition (route-scoped section components under `app/what-we-do/ai-strategy-roadmap/_components/`) that supplies this page's content and copy to those existing primitives.

### Key Entities

- **Capability**: One of the four service offerings shown in "CTO-as-a-Service capabilities" — has a discipline label/number, a title, a short lead description, and a list of supporting bullet points.
- **Engagement Stage**: One of the five ordered stages in "Diagnose. Roadmap. Execute. Measure. Coach." — has a sequence number, a title, and a description.
- **Value Proposition Tile**: One of the six items in "Why leaders choose TechGrit" — has an icon, a heading, and a description.
- **Advisory Segment Card**: One of the four items in "Founders. Boards. Scaling technology orgs." — has an icon, a name, and a description; none of the four carry a link, matching the reference.
- **FAQ Item**: One of the five question/answer pairs — has a question, an answer, and an independent expanded/collapsed state.
- **Related Service Link**: One of the six items in "Related services" — has an icon, a name, a short description, and a link to that service's page.
- **Hero Stat Tile**: One of the four stat tiles originally shown in the hero's right-side card (Advisors, Engagement, Roadmap, Outcomes) — retained as the source content that the replacement hero image visually represents, matching the sibling pages' equivalent treatment.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: A person familiar with the reference prototype, shown the implemented page side by side with the reference at the same viewport width, cannot identify a visually noticeable difference in any of the nine sections.
- **SC-002**: All page text matches the reference prototype at 100% fidelity — zero content substitutions, omissions, or additions when diffed against the reference's copy (excluding the hero stat tiles/caption removal covered by FR-004).
- **SC-003**: The page remains fully readable and free of horizontal overflow or overlapping elements at desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile widths.
- **SC-004**: A visitor can independently expand each of the 5 FAQ items and reach every one of the 4 capability cards, 5 engagement stages, 6 "why" tiles, 4 advisory-segment cards, and 6 related-service links without encountering a broken or dead interaction.
- **SC-005**: Both site-wide navigation entry points for this service ("What We Do" footer link and header mega-menu entry) resolve to the new page rather than the old `/services` anchor.

## Assumptions

- The reference prototype (`TechGrit AI Strategy.dc.html`) is the sixth of the seven "What We Do" sibling design prototypes in `raw-files-v3/TechGrit Website V2.3/` (per its own "Service 06 · CTO Advisory" hero eyebrow) and reuses the exact hero, content-block, capability-card, process-step, icon-tile, FAQ, and CTA-band markup structure the five already-built sibling pages already extracted into `components/ui/` (`Hero`, `ContentBlock`, `GlassCard`, `ProcessSteps`, `Faq`, `IconTile`, `final-cta`), differing only in copy, stat values, section counts (4 capabilities vs. the siblings' 6), and grid column counts. This is the basis for FR-011: no new shared primitive is needed for this page.
- **Static content only, CMS integration deferred**: All five existing "What We Do" sibling pages (AI-Accelerated Modernization, Software Product Engineering, Data & AI Engineering, Platform Engineering, Managed Services) have, as of this writing, each received their own separate, later CMS-integration ticket (fetching content from a live Strapi endpoint via `cms/api/what-we-do/*.ts`) — but every one of those tickets was itself preceded by an initial static-content build phase exactly like this one, and each subsequent sibling's own static-content ticket (Platform Engineering, Managed Services) explicitly re-confirmed targeting that same static-first phase even after earlier siblings had already been upgraded to CMS, on the grounds that provisioning a new Strapi content type/entry for a brand-new page slug is outside this codebase's scope. That same reasoning applies here: no Strapi content type/entry for an "ai-strategy-roadmap" page slug exists yet. Per Clarifications, this spec targets the static-content build phase — a page-local TypeScript content module (`_data/types.ts` + a page-local content constant), not a live CMS integration. CMS integration remains a separate, later follow-up ticket, exactly as it was for all five siblings (per FR-008).
- **Hero card image-replacement treatment**: Per Clarifications, the hero card's stat tiles and caption are dropped in favor of an image, following the five built siblings' precedent. Of the `public/samples/*` assets not yet consumed by a built "What We Do" or Industries page (`dm-copilot.png`, `dm-scalability.png`, and `ind-fintech.png` remain free — `svc-eng.png`, `svc-qa.png`, and `svc-uiux.png` are each claimed by a "What We Do" sibling, `ind-construction.png` by the Construction page, and `ind-healthcare.png` by Managed Services), `dm-scalability.png` ("Infinite Scalability" — architecture that adapts and scales) is used here: its growth/scale-trajectory motif reads coherently next to this page's "Roadmap" and long-horizon strategy messaging, and it keeps the `dm-*` bucket (already precedented for "What We Do" hero cards via `dm-tech-debt.png` on AI-Accelerated Modernization) separate from the `ind-*` bucket, preserving `ind-fintech.png` unclaimed for the still-unbuilt FinTech industry page rather than creating a second cross-bucket competition for it.
- The "Why leaders choose TechGrit" tile pattern is implemented as a page-local composition (not the shared `components/ui/IconTile`), matching the precedent already set by every sibling page's own equivalent "why" section, which made the same architectural choice for the same visual pattern.
- The "Founders. Boards. Scaling technology orgs." section reuses `GlassCard`'s `serviceCapability` variant on a four-column desktop track, preserving the reference's own fully-non-linked treatment (all four cards are `<div>`s, not `<a>`s) — a genuine content/structure fact from the reference, preserved as-is per the instruction to not alter reference content or structure.
- Destination links to sibling "What We Do" pages referenced by this page (AI-Accelerated Modernization, Software Product Engineering, Data & AI Engineering, Platform Engineering, Managed Services, Startups) point to those pages' intended routes regardless of whether each sibling page has been built yet (Startups has not); this page's own build does not block on their existence.
- "Pixel-perfect" is evaluated at the reference's authored desktop viewport; intermediate breakpoints follow the reference's documented responsive rules mapped onto this codebase's existing canonical breakpoints (`lg`=1140px, `md`=960px, `sm`=560px) rather than the reference's own literal inline breakpoints (1140px/920px/640px, which already align closely with the project's own breakpoints).
- Legal/placeholder footer links (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Preferences) inherit whatever the existing shared Footer already does site-wide; this feature does not alter Footer behavior.
- The new page lives at `/what-we-do/ai-strategy-roadmap` (`app/what-we-do/ai-strategy-roadmap/page.tsx`), following the exact routing and file/folder pattern already established by `app/what-we-do/ai-modernization/`, `app/what-we-do/software-product-engineering/`, `app/what-we-do/data-ai-engineering/`, `app/what-we-do/platform-engineering/`, and `app/what-we-do/managed-services/`. Today, both the footer's "What We Do" list and the header's live CMS-driven mega menu point this service at `/services#svc-strategy`, an anchor inside the combined Services page; FR-010 repoints both to the new route, mirroring the precedent already set for the five sibling entries in both files.
