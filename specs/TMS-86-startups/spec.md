## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Evaluate TechGrit as a startup engineering partner end-to-end (Priority: P1)

A founder or venture-backed technical lead evaluating whether TechGrit can be their engineering partner lands on the Startups page — from site navigation, a related "What We Do" page, or a direct link — and reads through the hero promise, the "great ideas stall" framing, the three-stage growth journey (Pre-Seed & Seed, Series A, Series B+), and the six capability areas, to decide whether TechGrit fits their stage and needs before reaching out.

**Why this priority**: This is the entire reason the page exists — it is TechGrit's primary sales asset for its startup-focused engineering service line. Without this flow working, the page delivers no value regardless of what else is built.

**Independent Test**: Load the page standalone and verify a reader can, without leaving the page, understand what TechGrit offers startups, see which of the three growth stages (Pre-Seed & Seed, Series A, Series B+) matches their own company, and see all six capability areas (Product Discovery & Validation, MVP & Prototype Development, AI-Native Product Engineering, Engineering Team & Platform, Maintenance & Support, Partnership & Network) with their supporting bullet points — this alone constitutes a usable, demonstrable page.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a visitor arrives on the page, **When** the hero section renders, **Then** they see the "Service 07 · Startups" eyebrow, the headline "Software product engineering for startups that move fast and build to last." (with "startups that move fast and build to last." rendered as the gradient-accented phrase), the supporting paragraph, both hero CTAs ("Talk to a Startup Advisor", "See capabilities"), and the right-side highlight card showing only a static image with no stat tiles or caption line — with the reference's original four stat tiles (Speed, Access, Models, Track Record) and its "AI-native architecture · OrbitAI™ · 4D™ frameworks" caption line not rendered anywhere on or around the card (per FR-004).
2. **Given** a visitor scrolls past the hero, **When** they reach the "Great ideas stall when engineering doesn't keep pace with your ambition." intro section, **Then** they see the section's heading and paragraph on one side and the "Challenges we solve for founders" chip list (six trigger chips) on the other.
3. **Given** a visitor reaches the "From first idea to institutional scale." section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see the three ordered growth-stage cards (Pre-Seed & Seed, Series A, Series B+) inside a single bordered panel, each with its stage badge, heading, lead paragraph, and bullet list, matching the reference verbatim.
4. **Given** a visitor reaches the "capabilities" section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see the section heading "Six capabilities. Every startup stage." (corrected per Clarifications to match the actual card count) followed by exactly six capability cards (Product Discovery & Validation, MVP & Prototype Development, AI-Native Product Engineering, Engineering Team & Platform, Maintenance & Support, and the bonus "+ Network" Partnership & Network card), each with its numbered/disciplined label ("01 · Discover" through "05 · Sustain", and "+ Network"), heading, lead paragraph, and bullet list.

### User Story 2 - Understand why the approach matters and who it's for (Priority: P2)

A visitor who is already interested in TechGrit but is evaluating *why TechGrit specifically* reads the "Senior engineers, startup speed, enterprise discipline." tiles and the "Founders, venture teams, and builders at every stage." cards to confirm TechGrit understands both their situation (runway-constrained, needing senior talent, AI-native) and has relevant experience with organizations like theirs (solo founders, seed/Series A teams, VC/PE portfolios, corporate innovation labs) before proceeding further.

**Why this priority**: This section builds the trust and differentiation needed to convert an already-interested visitor, but the page is still functional and valuable without a reader reaching this far (P1 covers the core pitch).

**Independent Test**: With only the hero, intro, growth-stage, and capabilities sections present, add the "why" tiles and "who we help" cards and verify each renders independently with correct content, without depending on any interactive state from earlier sections.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Why TechGrit for startups" section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see all six tiles (Senior developers from the start, AI-native by default, Flexible pricing for every stage, Track record of 40+ launches, We protect your runway, Network & ecosystem access) with icon, heading, and description.
2. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Founders, venture teams, and builders at every stage." section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see four non-clickable cards (Solo & Co-Founders, Seed & Series A Teams, VC & PE Portcos, Corporate Innovation) in that order, each with an icon, heading, and description, matching the reference's own plain (non-linked) card treatment for all four.

---

### User Story 3 - Resolve open questions and convert (Priority: P3)

A visitor with specific procedural concerns (pre-revenue eligibility, pricing models, technical co-founder support, how quickly work can start, investor introductions) expands the FAQ accordion to get direct answers, optionally checks related services, and then acts on a CTA (talks to a startup advisor or books a discovery sprint) or navigates to a related service page.

**Why this priority**: This closes remaining objections and drives the conversion action, but is only reached by visitors who have already engaged with P1/P2 content — it is the funnel's final step, not its entry point.

**Independent Test**: Render just the FAQ, related-services, and closing CTA sections and verify each FAQ item expands/collapses independently, related-service links are correct, and both CTA buttons point to their respective destinations.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the FAQ section is visible, **When** a visitor clicks a closed question, **Then** that question's answer expands in place (the first question, "Do you work with pre-revenue startups?", is expanded by default) without affecting the state of other questions.
2. **Given** the "Related services" section, **When** it renders, **Then** it shows six linked service cards in a three-across grid (Software Product Engineering, AI Strategy & Roadmap, Data & AI Engineering, AI-Accelerated Modernization, Platform Engineering, Managed Services).
3. **Given** the closing CTA band, **When** it renders, **Then** it shows the heading "Your idea deserves senior engineers, not excuses.", the supporting paragraph, and both the primary ("Talk to a Startup Advisor") and secondary ("Book a Discovery Sprint") actions.

### Edge Cases

- What happens on narrow viewports (mobile) where the hero's two-column layout, the two/three/four-across chip, growth-stage, capability, and "who we help" grids, and the six-across why grid cannot fit side by side? Each must collapse to a readable single- or two-column stack rather than overflowing horizontally or shrinking text below a legible size.
- What happens when a visitor navigates directly to `#capabilities` (the "See capabilities" hero link) via a bookmarked or shared URL? The target section must be reachable and not obscured by the sticky site header.
- What happens if a visitor expands multiple FAQ items in sequence? Each item's expand/collapse state must be independent of the others (no accordion-style forced-collapse of previously opened items).
- What happens to the "Founders, venture teams, and builders at every stage." cards, all four of which are non-interactive in the reference (no destination for any of Solo & Co-Founders, Seed & Series A Teams, VC & PE Portcos, or Corporate Innovation)? All four must render as plain, non-clickable cards (no hover-lift/pointer affordance implying a destination that doesn't exist), matching the reference's own use of a `<div>` card (not an `<a>`) for every card in this grid.
- What happens to the four hero stat tiles and the hero card's decorative treatment once the right-side content is replaced with an image, per FR-004? The card's size, position, border radius, and framing must remain visually equivalent to the reference even though its contents changed from stat tiles (plus a caption line) to a plain image with no caption, and the image must not distort or crop awkwardly at any supported viewport width.
- What happens to the "capabilities" section's heading, which reads "Five capabilities. Every startup stage." in the reference while the section renders six cards (five numbered plus the visually-distinguished "+ Network" bonus capability)? Per Clarifications, this is treated as a plain five-vs-six content/count error — the same class of mismatch corrected on the Data & AI Engineering sibling — so the rendered heading reads "Six capabilities. Every startup stage." while all six cards (including "+ Network") render unchanged from the reference.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The page MUST present all page copy — headings, eyebrows, paragraphs, card titles, bullet lists, chip labels, FAQ questions/answers, CTA labels, and link labels — exactly as written in the reference prototype, with no rewriting, rephrasing, shortening, expanding, reordering, or added/removed content (excluding the hero stat tiles/caption removal covered by FR-004, and the capabilities-heading count correction covered by FR-003a).
- **FR-002**: The page MUST reuse the site's existing shared Header and Footer components without modification or duplication of either component's code, and MUST reuse existing `components/ui/` primitives — `Hero`, `ContentBlock`, `GlassCard` (`serviceCapability` variant), `Faq`, `IconTile` (for the related-services compact cards), and `final-cta` — wherever their shape already matches this page's sections, in place of building new one-off equivalents.
- **FR-003**: The page MUST render, in reference order: hero, "Great ideas stall when engineering doesn't keep pace with your ambition." intro/chips block, three-stage "From first idea to institutional scale." growth-journey panel, six-capability "Six capabilities. Every startup stage." grid, six-tile "Senior engineers, startup speed, enterprise discipline." why grid, four-card "Founders, venture teams, and builders at every stage." grid, five-item FAQ accordion, six-card "Related services" grid, and closing CTA band.
- **FR-003a**: The capabilities section's heading MUST read "Six capabilities. Every startup stage." rather than the reference's literal "Five capabilities. Every startup stage." — per Clarifications, this is a deliberate correction of a five-vs-six count mismatch (the section renders six cards: five numbered capabilities plus the visually-distinguished "+ Network" bonus card), following the same correction precedent already applied on the Data & AI Engineering sibling page.
- **FR-004**: The hero section's right-side highlight card MUST display only a static image — `public/samples/ind-fintech.png` (per Clarifications and Assumptions) — with no caption line beneath it — with no rendering of the reference's four stat tiles (Speed, Access, Models, Track Record) or its "AI-native architecture · OrbitAI™ · 4D™ frameworks" caption line anywhere on or around the card — and without distorting the image's aspect ratio at any supported viewport width, matching the sibling "What We Do" pages' own hero-card image-replacement treatment (chrome, dimensions, position, border radius, spacing) via the shared `Hero` component's `mediaFill` media slot.
- **FR-005**: Every interactive affordance present in the reference MUST work in the implementation: each FAQ item independently expands/collapses (first item expanded by default); the "See capabilities" hero link scrolls to the capabilities section without that section being hidden under the sticky header; and all CTA, card, and related-service links navigate to their intended destination (or a page-appropriate equivalent where the destination does not yet exist as a built route).
- **FR-006**: The page's visual presentation (typography scale, weight, line-height, letter-spacing, colors, gradients, spacing, card/section dimensions, border radii, shadows, and icons) MUST match the reference prototype at the reference's desktop width, with no visually approximated values where the reference specifies an exact one.
- **FR-007**: The page's layout MUST adapt responsively across desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile widths, using the project's existing canonical breakpoints (`lg`=1140px, `md`=960px, `sm`=560px) to reproduce the reference's own breakpoint behavior (e.g., multi-column grids collapsing to fewer columns, then to a single column; the hero's two-column split stacking on narrow viewports) rather than a uniform shrink of the desktop layout or a newly invented pixel breakpoint.
- **FR-008**: This phase MUST implement static content only — no network requests, data fetching, CMS integration, or client-side data-derived state — while remaining structured (page-local `_data/types.ts` content shape, matching the sibling pages' own initial-build data organization) so that content can later be supplied dynamically without a rebuild of the page's visual structure, consistent with every "What We Do" page in this codebase having itself first been built statically before any CMS integration followed later as separate, dedicated work (per Assumptions).
- **FR-009**: No functional requirement in this specification requires modifying any page, component, or configuration outside what is needed to build this new page and the minimal navigation-pointer updates in FR-010.
- **FR-010**: The site's existing "What We Do → Startups" entry points MUST resolve to the new page at the route `/what-we-do/startups` once built. Concretely: (a) the footer's "What We Do" link config (`cms/api/footer.ts`) MUST have its "Startups" entry's `href` updated from `/services#svc-startups` to `/what-we-do/startups`; and (b) the header's mega-menu mapping (`cms/api/header.ts`'s `toMegaGroup`), which already special-cases the "AI-Accelerated Modernization", "Software Product Engineering", "Data and AI Engineering", "Platform Engineering", "Managed Services", and "AI Strategy & Roadmap" entries to override their stale CMS-supplied links, MUST gain an equivalent seventh special case for the "Startups" entry pointing to the same new route — following the exact precedent already established in that file. Live CMS content, the rest of the `/services` page, and all other navigation entries remain untouched.
- **FR-011**: This feature MUST NOT introduce any new shared `components/ui/` primitive. Every section this page needs (hero with its image-media treatment, intro/chips content block, capability card, related-service `IconTile`, FAQ accordion, and closing CTA band) already has a matching, unmodified `components/ui/` component established by the sibling "What We Do" pages; the three-stage growth-journey panel, the "why" tiles, and the fully-non-linked "who we help" cards are implemented as page-local compositions rather than new shared primitives, matching the exact precedent the sibling pages already set for their own equivalent sections (e.g., a bordered gradient panel wrapping `GlassCard` instances, matching the precedent already set by a sibling page's own bordered-panel section). This page's own build work is limited to page-local composition (route-scoped section components under `app/what-we-do/startups/_components/`) that supplies this page's content and copy to those existing primitives.

### Key Entities

- **Growth Stage**: One of the three ordered stages in "From first idea to institutional scale." (Pre-Seed & Seed, Series A, Series B+) — has a stage badge label, a title, a lead description, and a list of supporting bullet points.
- **Capability**: One of the six service offerings shown in the capabilities section — has a discipline label (a sequence number for the first five, or the distinguishing "+ Network" label for the sixth), a title, a short lead description, and a list of supporting bullet points.
- **Value Proposition Tile**: One of the six items in "Senior engineers, startup speed, enterprise discipline." — has an icon, a heading, and a description.
- **Founder Segment Card**: One of the four items in "Founders, venture teams, and builders at every stage." — has an icon, a name, and a description; none of the four carry a link, matching the reference.
- **FAQ Item**: One of the five question/answer pairs — has a question, an answer, and an independent expanded/collapsed state.
- **Related Service Link**: One of the six items in "Related services" — has an icon, a name, a short description, and a link to that service's page.
- **Hero Stat Tile**: One of the four stat tiles originally shown in the hero's right-side card (Speed, Access, Models, Track Record) — retained as the source content that the replacement hero image visually represents, matching the sibling pages' equivalent treatment.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: A person familiar with the reference prototype, shown the implemented page side by side with the reference at the same viewport width, cannot identify a visually noticeable difference in any of the nine sections.
- **SC-002**: All page text matches the reference prototype at 100% fidelity — zero content substitutions, omissions, or additions when diffed against the reference's copy (excluding the hero stat tiles/caption removal covered by FR-004).
- **SC-003**: The page remains fully readable and free of horizontal overflow or overlapping elements at desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile widths.
- **SC-004**: A visitor can independently expand each of the 5 FAQ items and reach every one of the 3 growth-stage cards, 6 capability cards, 6 "why" tiles, 4 founder-segment cards, and 6 related-service links without encountering a broken or dead interaction.
- **SC-005**: Both site-wide navigation entry points for this service ("What We Do" footer link and header mega-menu entry) resolve to the new page rather than the old `/services` anchor.

## Assumptions

- The reference prototype (`TechGrit Startups.dc.html`) is the seventh and final "What We Do" sibling design prototype in `raw-files-v3/TechGrit Website V2.3/` (per its own "Service 07 · Startups" hero eyebrow) and reuses the exact hero, content-block, capability-card, icon-tile, FAQ, and CTA-band markup structure the six already-built sibling pages already extracted into `components/ui/` (`Hero`, `ContentBlock`, `GlassCard`, `Faq`, `final-cta`), differing only in copy, stat values, section counts, and the addition of a bordered growth-journey panel not present on every sibling. This is the basis for FR-011: no new shared primitive is needed for this page.
- **Static content only, CMS integration deferred**: All six existing "What We Do" sibling pages (AI-Accelerated Modernization, Software Product Engineering, Data & AI Engineering, Platform Engineering, Managed Services, AI Strategy & Roadmap) have, as of this writing, each received their own separate, later CMS-integration ticket (fetching content from a live Strapi endpoint via `cms/api/what-we-do/*.ts`) — but every one of those tickets was itself preceded by an initial static-content build phase exactly like this one, and each subsequent sibling's own static-content ticket (Platform Engineering, Managed Services, AI Strategy & Roadmap) explicitly re-confirmed targeting that same static-first phase even after earlier siblings had already been upgraded to CMS, on the grounds that provisioning a new Strapi content type/entry for a brand-new page slug is outside this codebase's scope. That same reasoning applies here: no Strapi content type/entry for a "startups" page slug exists yet. Per Clarifications, this spec targets the static-content build phase — a page-local TypeScript content module (`_data/types.ts` + a page-local content constant), not a live CMS integration. CMS integration remains a separate, later follow-up ticket, exactly as it was for all six siblings (per FR-008).
- **Hero card image-replacement treatment**: Per Clarifications, the hero card's stat tiles and caption are dropped in favor of an image, following the six built siblings' precedent.
