## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Evaluate the managed services offering end-to-end (Priority: P1)

A prospective client (a CTO, VP of Engineering, or operations lead deciding whether to hand off application support and operations to a partner) lands on the Managed Services page — from site navigation, the homepage, or a direct link — and reads through the hero promise, the six service capabilities, and the five-stage operating lifecycle, to decide whether TechGrit's managed-services approach fits their needs before requesting contact.

**Why this priority**: This is the entire reason the page exists — it is TechGrit's primary sales asset for the managed-services line. Without this flow working, the page delivers no value regardless of what else is built.

**Independent Test**: Load the page standalone and verify a reader can, without leaving the page, understand what "managed services" means at TechGrit, see the six named capabilities (Support, Enhance, AIOps, Reliability, Cloud, Security) with their supporting bullet points, and see the five-stage lifecycle (Monitor, Detect, Resolve, Optimize, Evolve) — this alone constitutes a usable, demonstrable page.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a visitor arrives on the page, **When** the hero section renders, **Then** they see the "Service 05 · Managed Services" eyebrow, the headline "Keep your software reliable, secure, and continuously improving." (with "reliable, secure, and continuously improving." rendered as the gradient-accented phrase), the supporting paragraph, both hero CTAs ("Talk to Our Managed Services Team", "See capabilities"), and the right-side highlight card showing only a static image with no stat tiles or caption line — with the reference's original four stat tiles (Coverage, Detect, Resolve, Uptime) and its "OrbitAI™ AIOps · SRE-led operations" caption line not rendered anywhere on or around the card (per FR-004).
2. **Given** a visitor scrolls past the hero, **When** they reach the "Maintaining software shouldn't crowd out building software." intro section, **Then** they see the section's heading and paragraph on one side and the "What we hear from teams" chip list (six chips) on the other.
3. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Capabilities" section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see the section heading "Six capabilities. One always-on team." followed by exactly six capability cards (Application Support & Maintenance, Continuous Application Improvement, AI-Assisted Operations, Performance & Reliability Engineering, Cloud & Platform Operations, Security & Compliance Management), each with its numbered/disciplined label ("01 · Support" through "06 · Security"), heading, lead paragraph, and bullet list, matching the reference verbatim.
4. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Monitor. Detect. Resolve. Optimize. Evolve." section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see the five ordered stages (Monitor, Detect, Resolve, Optimize, Evolve) each with its number, title, and description.

---

### User Story 2 - Understand why the approach matters and see relevant application experience (Priority: P2)

A visitor who is already sold on the need for ongoing application support is evaluating *why TechGrit specifically* reads the "Not ticket-closing — outcomes." tiles and the "Any stack. Any origin. Any scale." industry cards to confirm TechGrit understands both the operational outcomes that matter (reliability, reduced overhead, faster delivery) and has relevant domain experience (HealthTech, FinTech, Construction Tech) before proceeding further.

**Why this priority**: This section builds the trust and differentiation needed to convert an already-interested visitor, but the page is still functional and valuable without a reader reaching this far (P1 covers the core pitch).

**Independent Test**: With only the hero, intro, capabilities, and lifecycle sections present, add the "why" tiles and industry cards and verify each renders independently with correct content and links, without depending on any interactive state from earlier sections.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Why choose TechGrit" section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see all six tiles (Improve reliability, Reduce operational overhead, Accelerate delivery, Lower technical debt, Extend engineering, Enable continuous modernization) with icon, heading, and description.
2. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Applications we support" section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see three linked industry cards (HealthTech, FinTech, Construction Tech) in that order, each navigating to its respective industry page.

---

### User Story 3 - Resolve open questions and convert (Priority: P3)

A visitor with specific procedural concerns (how managed services differ from traditional support, whether TechGrit only supports apps it built, whether new feature work is included, 24×7 coverage, and onboarding an existing application) expands the FAQ accordion to get direct answers, optionally checks related services, and then acts on a CTA (talks to the managed services team or schedules an assessment) or navigates to a related service page.

**Why this priority**: This closes remaining objections and drives the conversion action, but is only reached by visitors who have already engaged with P1/P2 content — it is the funnel's final step, not its entry point.

**Independent Test**: Render just the FAQ, related-services, and closing CTA sections and verify each FAQ item expands/collapses independently, related-service links are correct, and both CTA buttons point to their respective destinations.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the FAQ section is visible, **When** a visitor clicks a closed question, **Then** that question's answer expands in place (the first question, "How are your Managed Services different from traditional support?", is expanded by default) without affecting the state of other questions.
2. **Given** the "Related services" section, **When** it renders, **Then** it shows six linked service cards (AI-Accelerated Modernization, Software Product Engineering, Data & AI Engineering, Platform Engineering, AI Strategy & Roadmap, Startups).
3. **Given** the closing CTA band, **When** it renders, **Then** it shows the heading "Managed services that evolve your software — not just maintain it.", the supporting paragraph, and both the primary ("Schedule a Managed Services Assessment") and secondary ("Explore engagement models") actions.

---

### Edge Cases

- What happens on narrow viewports (mobile) where the hero's two-column layout, the three-across capability grid, the five-across lifecycle strip, the two-across "why" grid, the three-across industry grid, and the three-across related-services grid cannot fit side by side? Each must collapse to a readable single- or two-column stack rather than overflowing horizontally or shrinking text below a legible size.
- What happens when a visitor navigates directly to `#capabilities` (the "See capabilities" hero link) via a bookmarked or shared URL? The target section must be reachable and not obscured by the sticky site header.
- What happens if a visitor expands multiple FAQ items in sequence? Each item's expand/collapse state must be independent of the others (no accordion-style forced-collapse of previously opened items).
- What happens to the hero card's four stat tiles and caption line once the right-side content is replaced with an image, per FR-004 and the Clarifications answer? The card's size, position, border radius, and framing must remain visually equivalent to the reference even though its contents changed from stat tiles plus a caption line to a plain image with no caption, and the image must not distort or crop awkwardly at any supported viewport width.
- What happens with the reference's "Applications we support" grid, whose CSS declares a 4-column track (`grid-template-columns:repeat(4,1fr)`) while only 3 industry cards (HealthTech, FinTech, Construction Tech) actually populate it? Rendered literally, this would leave one empty trailing column on desktop. Per Assumptions, this is treated as a 3-column grid matching the 3 real cards — consistent with every other "Industries" grid already built for the sibling "What We Do" pages using this same set of 3 industries — rather than reproducing an empty fourth track.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The page MUST present all page copy — headings, eyebrows, paragraphs, card titles, bullet lists, FAQ questions/answers, CTA labels, and link labels — exactly as written in the reference prototype, with no rewriting, rephrasing, shortening, expanding, reordering, or added/removed content (excluding the hero stat tiles/caption removal covered by FR-004).
- **FR-002**: The page MUST reuse the site's existing shared Header and Footer components without modification or duplication of either component's code, and MUST reuse existing `components/ui/` primitives — `Hero`, `ContentBlock`, `GlassCard` (`serviceCapability` variant), `ProcessSteps`, `Faq`, and `IconTile` (for the related-services compact cards) — wherever their shape already matches this page's sections, in place of building new one-off equivalents.
- **FR-003**: The page MUST render, in reference order: hero, "Maintaining software shouldn't crowd out building software." intro/chips block, six-capability "Six capabilities. One always-on team." grid, five-stage "Monitor. Detect. Resolve. Optimize. Evolve." lifecycle strip, six-tile "Not ticket-closing — outcomes." grid, three-card "Any stack. Any origin. Any scale." industry grid, five-item FAQ accordion, six-card "Related services" grid, and closing CTA band.
- **FR-004**: The hero section's right-side highlight card MUST display only a static image — `public/samples/ind-healthcare.png` (per Clarifications) — with no caption line beneath it — with no rendering of the reference's four stat tiles (Coverage, Detect, Resolve, Uptime) or its "OrbitAI™ AIOps · SRE-led operations" caption line anywhere on or around the card — and without distorting the image's aspect ratio at any supported viewport width, matching the sibling "What We Do" pages' own hero-card image-replacement treatment (chrome, dimensions, position, border radius, spacing) via the shared `Hero` component's `media` slot.
- **FR-005**: Every interactive affordance present in the reference MUST work in the implementation: each FAQ item independently expands/collapses (first item expanded by default); the "See capabilities" hero link scrolls to the capabilities section without that section being hidden under the sticky header; and all CTA, card, and related-service links navigate to their intended destination (or a page-appropriate equivalent where the destination does not yet exist as a built route).
- **FR-006**: The page's visual presentation (typography scale, weight, line-height, letter-spacing, colors, gradients, spacing, card/section dimensions, border radii, shadows, and icons) MUST match the reference prototype at the reference's desktop width, with no visually approximated values where the reference specifies an exact one.
- **FR-007**: The page's layout MUST adapt responsively across desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile widths, using the project's existing canonical breakpoints (`lg`=1140px, `md`=960px, `sm`=560px) to reproduce the reference's own breakpoint behavior (e.g., multi-column grids collapsing to fewer columns, then to a single column; the hero's two-column split stacking on narrow viewports) rather than a uniform shrink of the desktop layout or a newly invented pixel breakpoint.
- **FR-008**: This phase MUST implement static content only — no network requests, data fetching, CMS integration, or client-side data-derived state — while remaining structured (page-local `_data/types.ts` content shape, matching the sibling pages' own initial-build data organization) so that content can later be supplied dynamically without a rebuild of the page's visual structure, consistent with how every "What We Do" page in this codebase was itself first built statically before any CMS integration followed later as separate, dedicated work (per Assumptions).
- **FR-009**: No functional requirement in this specification requires modifying any page, component, or configuration outside what is needed to build this new page and the minimal navigation-pointer updates in FR-010.
- **FR-010**: The site's existing "What We Do → Managed Services" entry points MUST resolve to the new page at the route `/what-we-do/managed-services` once built. Concretely: (a) the footer's "What We Do" link config (`cms/api/footer.ts`) MUST have its "Managed Services" entry's `href` updated from `/services#svc-managed` to `/what-we-do/managed-services`; and (b) the header's mega-menu mapping (`cms/api/header.ts`'s `toMegaGroup`), which already special-cases the "AI-Accelerated Modernization", "Software Product Engineering", "Data and AI Engineering", and "Platform Engineering" entries to override their stale CMS-supplied links, MUST gain an equivalent fifth special case for the "Managed Services" entry pointing to the same new route — following the exact precedent already established in that file. Live CMS content, the rest of the `/services` page, and all other navigation entries remain untouched.
- **FR-011**: This feature MUST NOT introduce any new shared `components/ui/` primitive. Every section this page needs (hero with its image-media treatment, intro/chips content block, capability card, numbered process step, icon/text tile, linked industry card, FAQ accordion, and closing CTA band) already has a matching, unmodified `components/ui/` component established by the sibling "What We Do" pages; the "Why choose TechGrit" tiles are implemented as a page-local composition, matching the exact precedent the sibling pages already set for their own equivalent "why" sections. This page's own build work is limited to page-local composition (route-scoped section components under `app/what-we-do/managed-services/_components/`) that supplies this page's content and copy to those existing primitives.

### Key Entities

- **Capability**: One of the six service offerings shown in "Capabilities" — has a discipline label/number, a title, a short lead description, and a list of supporting bullet points.
- **Lifecycle Stage**: One of the five ordered stages in "Monitor. Detect. Resolve. Optimize. Evolve." — has a sequence number, a title, and a description.
- **Value Proposition Tile**: One of the six items in "Why choose TechGrit" — has an icon, a heading, and a description.
- **Industry Card**: One of the three items in "Applications we support" — has an icon, a name, a description, and a link to that industry's page.
- **FAQ Item**: One of the five question/answer pairs — has a question, an answer, and an independent expanded/collapsed state.
- **Related Service Link**: One of the six items in "Related services" — has an icon, a name, a short description, and a link to that service's page.
- **Hero Stat Tile**: One of the four stat tiles originally shown in the hero's right-side card (Coverage, Detect, Resolve, Uptime) — retained as the source content that the replacement hero image visually stands in for, matching the sibling pages' equivalent treatment.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: A person familiar with the reference prototype, shown the implemented page side by side with the reference at the same viewport width, cannot identify a visually noticeable difference in any of the nine sections.
- **SC-002**: All page text matches the reference prototype at 100% fidelity — zero content substitutions, omissions, or additions when diffed against the reference's copy (excluding the hero stat tiles/caption removal covered by FR-004).
- **SC-003**: The page remains fully readable and free of horizontal overflow or overlapping elements at desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile widths.
- **SC-004**: A visitor can independently expand each of the 5 FAQ items and reach every one of the 6 capability cards, 5 lifecycle stages, 6 "why" tiles, 3 linked industry cards, and 6 related-service links without encountering a broken or dead interaction.
- **SC-005**: Both site-wide navigation entry points for this service ("What We Do" footer link and header mega-menu entry) resolve to the new page rather than the old `/services` anchor.

## Assumptions

- The reference prototype (`TechGrit Managed Services.dc.html`) is the fifth of the seven "What We Do" sibling design prototypes already present in `raw-files-v3/TechGrit Website V2.3/` (per its own "Service 05 · Managed Services" hero eyebrow). It reuses the exact hero, content-block, capability-card, process-step, icon-tile, FAQ, and CTA-band markup structure the sibling AI-Accelerated Modernization, Software Product Engineering, Data & AI Engineering, and Platform Engineering pages already extracted into `components/ui/` (`Hero`, `ContentBlock`, `GlassCard`, `ProcessSteps`, `Faq`, `IconTile`, `final-cta`), differing only in copy, stat values, and card counts. This is the basis for FR-011: no new shared primitive is needed for this page.
- **Static content only, CMS integration deferred**: All four existing "What We Do" sibling pages have, as of this writing, since received their own separate CMS-integration follow-up work (fetching content from a live Strapi endpoint via `cms/api/what-we-do/*.ts`), mirroring the same two-phase pattern (static build, then a later dedicated CMS-integration ticket) already used for every other page in this codebase — a pattern the Data & AI Engineering and Platform Engineering tickets each independently confirmed via clarification when they hit this exact same fork. This spec targets that same initial, static-content build phase for Managed Services — a page-local TypeScript content module (`_data/types.ts` + a page-local content constant), not a live CMS integration — since no Strapi content type/entry for a "managed-services" page slug exists yet and provisioning one is outside this codebase's scope. CMS integration remains a separate, later follow-up ticket, exactly as it was for all four siblings (per FR-008).
- **Hero card image-replacement treatment**: Per the Clarifications answers, the hero card's stat tiles and caption are dropped in favor of an image, following the three built siblings' precedent, even though the shared `public/samples/svc-*.png` bucket those siblings drew from (`svc-eng.png`, `svc-qa.png`, `svc-uiux.png`) is now fully claimed. `public/samples/ind-healthcare.png` — an abstract pulse/heartbeat line-graph illustration, currently unused by any built page — is used instead: it is not tied to this page's subject matter, but its "system health" pulse-line motif reads coherently next to Managed Services' monitoring/reliability copy, and it avoids introducing a new image asset into the project.
- The "Why choose TechGrit" tile pattern is implemented as a page-local composition (not the shared `components/ui/IconTile`), matching the precedent already set by the sibling pages' own equivalent "why" sections, which made the same architectural choice for the same visual pattern.
- The "Applications we support" section reuses `GlassCard`'s `serviceCapability` variant (or the equivalent linked-card treatment already used by the sibling pages' own "Industries" sections) on a three-column desktop track. The reference's own grid CSS (`grid-template-columns:repeat(4,1fr)`) is treated as a template artifact rather than an intentional fourth column, since only 3 industry cards (HealthTech, FinTech, Construction Tech) are present in the reference markup — the same 3 industries every other sibling "Industries" section already renders in 3 columns — and all 3 cards are linked (no mixed link/no-link treatment, unlike Platform Engineering's distinct 4-card case).
- Destination links to sibling "What We Do" / Industries / How We Work pages referenced by this page (e.g., AI-Accelerated Modernization, Software Product Engineering, Data & AI Engineering, Platform Engineering, AI Strategy & Roadmap, Startups, HealthTech, FinTech, Construction Tech, Engagement Models) point to those pages' intended routes regardless of whether each sibling page has been built yet; this page's own build does not block on their existence.
- "Pixel-perfect" is evaluated at the reference's authored desktop viewport; intermediate breakpoints follow the reference's documented responsive rules mapped onto this codebase's existing canonical breakpoints (`lg`=1140px, `md`=960px, `sm`=560px) rather than the reference's own literal inline breakpoints (1140px/920px/640px, which already align closely with the project's own breakpoints).
- Legal/placeholder footer links (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Preferences) inherit whatever the existing shared Footer already does site-wide; this feature does not alter Footer behavior.
- The new page lives at `/what-we-do/managed-services` (`app/what-we-do/managed-services/page.tsx`), following the exact routing and file/folder pattern already established by `app/what-we-do/ai-modernization/`, `app/what-we-do/software-product-engineering/`, `app/what-we-do/data-ai-engineering/`, and `app/what-we-do/platform-engineering/`. Today, both the footer's "What We Do" list and the header's live CMS-driven mega menu point this service at `/services#svc-managed`, an anchor inside the combined Services page; FR-010 repoints both to the new route, mirroring the precedent already set for the four sibling entries in both files.
