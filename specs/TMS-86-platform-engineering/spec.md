## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Evaluate the platform engineering offering end-to-end (Priority: P1)

A prospective client (a CTO, VP of Engineering, or platform lead evaluating whether to invest in a dedicated platform team or partner) lands on the Platform Engineering page — from site navigation, the homepage, or a direct link — and reads through the hero promise, the six platform capabilities, and the five-stage delivery journey, to decide whether TechGrit's platform-engineering approach fits their needs before requesting contact.

**Why this priority**: This is the entire reason the page exists — it is TechGrit's primary sales asset for the platform engineering service line. Without this flow working, the page delivers no value regardless of what else is built.

**Independent Test**: Load the page standalone and verify a reader can, without leaving the page, understand what "platform engineering" means at TechGrit, see the six named capabilities (Strategy, Cloud-native, DevOps, IaC, Reliability, Security) with their supporting bullet points, and see the five-stage journey (Assess, Design, Build, Secure, Optimize) — this alone constitutes a usable, demonstrable page.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a visitor arrives on the page, **When** the hero section renders, **Then** they see the "Service 04 · Platform Engineering" eyebrow, the headline "Build intelligent platforms that accelerate software delivery." (with "accelerate software delivery." rendered as the gradient-accented phrase), the supporting paragraph, both hero CTAs ("Talk to a Platform Engineering Expert", "See capabilities"), and the right-side highlight card showing only the replacement image with no caption line beneath it — with the reference's original four stat tiles (Deploy freq, MTTR, Uptime, FinOps) and the reference's "PRISM™ · AI IMPACT™ · OrbitAI™ frameworks" caption line not rendered anywhere on or around the card (per FR-004).
2. **Given** a visitor scrolls past the hero, **When** they reach the "Stop solving the same infrastructure problem in every team." intro section, **Then** they see the section's heading and paragraph on one side and the "Signals you need a platform" chip list (six signal chips) on the other.
3. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Capabilities" section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see the section heading "Six pillars. One reliable foundation." followed by exactly six capability cards (Platform Strategy & Architecture, Cloud-Native Platform Engineering, DevOps & Intelligent CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code, Platform Reliability & Operations, Security & Platform Governance), each with its numbered/disciplined label ("01 · Strategy" through "06 · Security"), heading, lead paragraph, and bullet list, matching the reference verbatim.
4. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Assess. Design. Build. Secure. Optimize." section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see the five ordered stages (Assess, Design, Build, Secure, Optimize) each with its number, title, and description.

### User Story 2 - Understand why the approach matters and see relevant platform fit (Priority: P2)

A visitor who is already sold on the need for platform help but is evaluating *why TechGrit specifically* reads the "The best platform is invisible — and everywhere." tiles and the "Platforms for every stage of growth" cards to confirm TechGrit understands both generic platform archetypes (SaaS, Enterprise) and has relevant domain experience (HealthTech, FinTech) before proceeding further.

**Why this priority**: This section builds the trust and differentiation needed to convert an already-interested visitor, but the page is still functional and valuable without a reader reaching this far (P1 covers the core pitch).

**Independent Test**: With only the hero, intro, capabilities, and lifecycle sections present, add the "Why" tiles and "Platforms for every stage of growth" cards and verify each renders independently with correct content and links, without depending on any interactive state from earlier sections.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Why platform engineering matters" section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see all six tiles (Improve developer productivity, Accelerate delivery, Increase reliability, Standardize practices, Scale with confidence, AI-augmented ops) with icon, heading, and description.
2. **Given** a visitor reaches the "Platforms for every stage of growth" section, **When** it renders, **Then** they see four cards (SaaS Platforms, Enterprise Apps, HealthTech, FinTech) in that order, where the first two (SaaS Platforms, Enterprise Apps) render as static, non-clickable cards exactly as in the reference, and the last two (HealthTech, FinTech) are linked cards navigating to that industry's page.

---

### User Story 3 - Resolve open questions and convert (Priority: P3)

A visitor with specific procedural concerns (what platform engineering is, how it differs from DevOps, modernizing an existing platform, multi-cloud support, measuring platform success) expands the FAQ accordion to get direct answers, optionally checks related services, and then acts on a CTA (talks to a platform expert or books a discovery sprint) or navigates to a related service page.

**Why this priority**: This closes remaining objections and drives the conversion action, but is only reached by visitors who have already engaged with P1/P2 content — it is the funnel's final step, not its entry point.

**Independent Test**: Render just the FAQ, related-services, and closing CTA sections and verify each FAQ item expands/collapses independently, related-service links are correct, and both CTA buttons point to their respective destinations.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** the FAQ section is visible, **When** a visitor clicks a closed question, **Then** that question's answer expands in place (the first question, "What is Platform Engineering?", is expanded by default) without affecting the state of other questions.
2. **Given** the "Related services" section, **When** it renders, **Then** it shows six linked service cards (AI-Accelerated Modernization, Software Product Engineering, Data & AI Engineering, Managed Services, AI Strategy & Roadmap, Startups).
3. **Given** the closing CTA band, **When** it renders, **Then** it shows the heading "Cloud-native. Secure. Loved by developers.", the supporting paragraph, and both the primary ("Talk to a Platform Expert") and secondary ("Book a Discovery Sprint") actions.

### Edge Cases

- What happens on narrow viewports (mobile) where the hero's two-column layout, the three/six-across capability and why grids, the four-across "Platforms for every stage of growth" grid, and the five-across lifecycle strip cannot fit side by side? Each must collapse to a readable single- or two-column stack rather than overflowing horizontally or shrinking text below a legible size.
- What happens when a visitor navigates directly to `#capabilities` (the "See capabilities" hero link) via a bookmarked or shared URL? The target section must be reachable and not obscured by the sticky site header.
- What happens if a visitor expands multiple FAQ items in sequence? Each item's expand/collapse state must be independent of the others (no accordion-style forced-collapse of previously opened items).
- What happens in the "Platforms for every stage of growth" grid, which mixes two non-interactive cards (SaaS Platforms, Enterprise Apps — no destination) with two linked industry cards (HealthTech, FinTech)? The two non-linked cards must render as plain, non-clickable cards (no hover-lift/pointer affordance implying a destination that doesn't exist) while the two linked cards behave like every other linked capability/industry card in the site's design system, matching the reference's own distinction between a `<div>` card and an `<a>` card.
- What happens to the four hero stat tiles and the hero card's decorative treatment once the right-side content is replaced with an image, per FR-004? The card's size, position, border radius, and framing must remain visually equivalent to the reference even though its contents changed from stat tiles (plus a caption line) to a plain image with no caption, and the image must not distort or crop awkwardly at any supported viewport width.

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

### Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: The page MUST present all page copy — headings, eyebrows, paragraphs, card titles, bullet lists, FAQ questions/answers, CTA labels, and link labels — exactly as written in the reference prototype, with no rewriting, rephrasing, shortening, expanding, reordering, or added/removed content.
- **FR-002**: The page MUST reuse the site's existing shared Header and Footer components without modification or duplication of either component's code, and MUST reuse existing `components/ui/` primitives — `Hero`, `ContentBlock`, `GlassCard` (`serviceCapability` variant), `ProcessSteps`, `Faq`, `IconTile` (for the related-services compact cards), and `final-cta` — wherever their shape already matches this page's sections, in place of building new one-off equivalents.
- **FR-003**: The page MUST render, in reference order: hero, "Stop solving the same infrastructure problem in every team." intro/signals block, six-capability "Six pillars. One reliable foundation." grid, five-stage "Assess. Design. Build. Secure. Optimize." lifecycle strip, six-tile "The best platform is invisible — and everywhere." grid, four-card "SaaS to enterprise — sized to your team." grid, five-item FAQ accordion, six-card "Related services" grid, and closing CTA band.
- **FR-004**: The hero section's right-side highlight card MUST display only a static image (the next available unused `public/samples/svc-*` asset, `svc-uiux.png`) with no caption line beneath it — with no rendering of the reference's four stat tiles (Deploy freq, MTTR, Uptime, FinOps) or its "PRISM™ · AI IMPACT™ · OrbitAI™ frameworks" caption line anywhere on or around the card — and without distorting the image's aspect ratio at any supported viewport width, matching the sibling "What We Do" pages' own hero-card image-replacement treatment (chrome, dimensions, position, border radius, spacing) via the shared `Hero` component's `media` slot (the `mediaCaption` slot unused, matching Software Product Engineering's own treatment).
- **FR-005**: Every interactive affordance present in the reference MUST work in the implementation: each FAQ item independently expands/collapses (first item expanded by default); the "See capabilities" hero link scrolls to the capabilities section without that section being hidden under the sticky header; all CTA, card, and related-service links navigate to their intended destination (or a page-appropriate equivalent where the destination does not yet exist as a built route); and the "SaaS Platforms"/"Enterprise Apps" cards in the "Platforms for every stage of growth" grid remain non-interactive (no link) while "HealthTech"/"FinTech" link to their respective industry pages.
- **FR-006**: The page's visual presentation (typography scale, weight, line-height, letter-spacing, colors, gradients, spacing, card/section dimensions, border radii, shadows, and icons) MUST match the reference prototype at the reference's desktop width, with no visually approximated values where the reference specifies an exact one.
- **FR-007**: The page's layout MUST adapt responsively across desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile widths, using the project's existing canonical breakpoints (`lg`=1140px, `md`=960px, `sm`=560px) to reproduce the reference's own breakpoint behavior (e.g., multi-column grids collapsing to fewer columns, then to a single column; the hero's two-column split stacking on narrow viewports) rather than a uniform shrink of the desktop layout or a newly invented pixel breakpoint.
- **FR-008**: This phase MUST implement static content only — no network requests, data fetching, CMS integration, or client-side data-derived state — while remaining structured (page-local `_data/types.ts` content shape, matching the sibling pages' own initial-build data organization) so that content can later be supplied dynamically without a rebuild of the page's visual structure, consistent with how every "What We Do" page in this codebase was itself first built statically before any CMS integration followed later as separate, dedicated work (per Assumptions).
- **FR-009**: No functional requirement in this specification requires modifying any page, component, or configuration outside what is needed to build this new page and the minimal navigation-pointer updates in FR-010.
- **FR-010**: The site's existing "What We Do → Platform Engineering" entry points MUST resolve to the new page at the route `/what-we-do/platform-engineering` once built. Concretely: (a) the footer's "What We Do" link config (`cms/api/footer.ts`) MUST have its "Platform Engineering" entry's `href` updated from `/services#svc-platform` to `/what-we-do/platform-engineering`; and (b) the header's mega-menu mapping (`cms/api/header.ts`'s `toMegaGroup`), which already special-cases the "AI-Accelerated Modernization", "Software Product Engineering", and "Data and AI Engineering" entries to override their stale CMS-supplied links, MUST gain an equivalent fourth special case for the "Platform Engineering" entry pointing to the same new route — following the exact precedent already established in that file. Live CMS content, the rest of the `/services` page, and all other navigation entries remain untouched.
- **FR-011**: This feature MUST NOT introduce any new shared `components/ui/` primitive. Every section this page needs (hero with its image-media treatment, intro/signals content block, capability card, numbered process step, icon/text tile, FAQ accordion, and closing CTA band) already has a matching, unmodified `components/ui/` component established by the sibling "What We Do" pages; the "Why platform engineering matters" tiles and the mixed link/no-link "Platforms for every stage of growth" cards are implemented as page-local compositions rather than new shared primitives, matching the exact precedent both sibling pages already set for their own equivalent sections. This page's own build work is limited to page-local composition (route-scoped section components under `app/what-we-do/platform-engineering/_components/`) that supplies this page's content and copy to those existing primitives.

### Key Entities

- **Capability**: One of the six service offerings shown in "Capabilities" — has a discipline label/number, a title, a short lead description, and a list of supporting bullet points.
- **Lifecycle Stage**: One of the five ordered stages in "Assess. Design. Build. Secure. Optimize." — has a sequence number, a title, and a description.
- **Value Proposition Tile**: One of the six items in "Why platform engineering matters" — has an icon, a heading, and a description.
- **Platform/Industry Card**: One of the four items in "Platforms for every stage of growth" — has an icon, a name, a description, and an optional link to that industry's page (only present for HealthTech and FinTech; absent for SaaS Platforms and Enterprise Apps).
- **FAQ Item**: One of the five question/answer pairs — has a question, an answer, and an independent expanded/collapsed state.
- **Related Service Link**: One of the six items in "Related services" — has an icon, a name, a short description, and a link to that service's page.
- **Hero Stat Tile**: One of the four stat tiles originally shown in the hero's right-side card (Deploy freq, MTTR, Uptime, FinOps) — retained as the source content that the replacement hero image visually represents, matching the sibling pages' equivalent treatment.

## Success Criteria *(mandatory)*

### Measurable Outcomes

- **SC-001**: A person familiar with the reference prototype, shown the implemented page side by side with the reference at the same viewport width, cannot identify a visually noticeable difference in any of the nine sections.
- **SC-002**: All page text matches the reference prototype at 100% fidelity — zero content substitutions, omissions, or additions when diffed against the reference's copy.
- **SC-003**: The page remains fully readable and free of horizontal overflow or overlapping elements at desktop, laptop, tablet, and mobile widths.
- **SC-004**: A visitor can independently expand each of the 5 FAQ items and reach every one of the 6 capability cards, 5 lifecycle stages, 6 "why" tiles, 2 linked platform/industry cards, and 6 related-service links without encountering a broken or dead interaction.
- **SC-005**: Both site-wide navigation entry points for this service ("What We Do" footer link and header mega-menu entry) resolve to the new page rather than the old `/services` anchor.

## Assumptions

- The reference prototype (`TechGrit Platform Engineering.dc.html`) is the fourth of the seven "What We Do" sibling design prototypes already present in `raw-files-v3/TechGrit Website V2.3/` (per its own "Service 04 · Platform Engineering" hero eyebrow). It reuses the exact hero, content-block, capability-card, process-step, icon-tile, FAQ, and CTA-band markup structure the sibling AI-Accelerated Modernization, Software Product Engineering, and Data & AI Engineering pages already extracted into `components/ui/` (`Hero`, `ContentBlock`, `GlassCard`, `ProcessSteps`, `Faq`, `IconTile`, `final-cta`), differing only in copy, stat values, and the mixed link/no-link treatment of its fourth grid section. This is the basis for FR-011: no new shared primitive is needed for this page.
- **Static content only, CMS integration deferred**: All three existing "What We Do" sibling pages have, as of this writing, since received their own separate CMS-integration follow-up work (fetching content from a live Strapi endpoint via `cms/api/what-we-do/*.ts`), mirroring the same two-phase pattern (static build, then a later dedicated CMS-integration ticket) already used for every other page in this codebase — including the Data & AI Engineering page's own explicitly-confirmed initial phase (`specs/TMS-86-data-and-ai-engineering/`). This spec targets that same initial, static-content build phase for Platform Engineering — a page-local TypeScript content module (`_data/types.ts` + a page-local content constant), not a live CMS integration — since no Strapi content type/entry for a "platform-engineering" page slug exists yet and provisioning one is outside this codebase's scope. CMS integration remains a separate, later follow-up ticket, exactly as it was for all three siblings (per FR-008).
- **Hero card image-replacement treatment**: All three sibling pages replaced their hero card's stat-tile content with a static image. This page follows the same treatment. Of the `public/samples/svc-*` naming bucket the siblings' own hero images came from (`svc-eng.png` used by Software Product Engineering, `svc-qa.png` used by Data & AI Engineering), `svc-uiux.png` is the one remaining unused asset in that bucket and is used here as the next candidate, following the same "first remaining candidate in the naming bucket" precedent already established when Data & AI Engineering chose `svc-qa.png`. Per Clarifications, the caption line ("PRISM™ · AI IMPACT™ · OrbitAI™ frameworks") is dropped, matching Software Product Engineering's own treatment (the other two siblings kept an equivalent caption).
- The "Why platform engineering matters" tile pattern is implemented as a page-local composition (not the shared `components/ui/IconTile`), matching the precedent already set by both sibling pages' own equivalent "why" sections, which made the same architectural choice for the same visual pattern.
- The "Platforms for every stage of growth" section reuses `GlassCard`'s `serviceCapability` variant on a four-column desktop track, preserving the reference's own mixed treatment of two non-linked generic cards (SaaS Platforms, Enterprise Apps) alongside two linked industry cards (HealthTech, FinTech) — a genuine, intentional content/structure difference from the sibling pages' own "Industries" sections (which link every card), preserved as-is per the instruction to not alter reference content or structure.
- Destination links to sibling "What We Do" / Industries / How We Work pages referenced by this page (e.g., AI-Accelerated Modernization, Software Product Engineering, Data & AI Engineering, Managed Services, AI Strategy & Roadmap, Startups, HealthTech, FinTech, Discovery Sprints) point to those pages' intended routes regardless of whether each sibling page has been built yet; this page's own build does not block on their existence.
- "Pixel-perfect" is evaluated at the reference's authored desktop viewport; intermediate breakpoints follow the reference's documented responsive rules mapped onto this codebase's existing canonical breakpoints (`lg`=1140px, `md`=960px, `sm`=560px) rather than the reference's own literal inline breakpoints (1140px/920px/640px, which already align closely with the project's own breakpoints).
- Legal/placeholder footer links (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Cookie Preferences) inherit whatever the existing shared Footer already does site-wide; this feature does not alter Footer behavior.
- The new page lives at `/what-we-do/platform-engineering` (`app/what-we-do/platform-engineering/page.tsx`), following the exact routing and file/folder pattern already established by `app/what-we-do/ai-modernization/`, `app/what-we-do/software-product-engineering/`, and `app/what-we-do/data-ai-engineering/`. Today, both the footer's "What We Do" list and the header's live CMS-driven mega menu point this service at `/services#svc-platform`, an anchor inside the combined Services page; FR-010 repoints both to the new route, mirroring the precedent already set for the three sibling entries in both files.
